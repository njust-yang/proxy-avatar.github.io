Proxy Avatar Meets Low-Rank Caching: Real-Time One-Shot Emotion-Controllable Portrait Animation

Haijie Yang1, Jindi Bao1, Yixuan Dong2, Hongliang Zhang1, Jian Bi1,
1-PCALab of Nanjing University of Science and Technology
2-Tsientang Institute for Advanced Study
Code arXiv

Abstract

Audio-driven portrait animation faces a persistent trade-off between expressive emotion control and real-time one-shot generation. Diffusion-based methods can synthesize high-quality portraits, but they repeatedly compute reference appearance features during denoising and usually lack an explicit source of emotion-aware facial motion. We propose Proxy Avatar Meets Low-Rank Caching, a cascaded framework that separates emotional motion generation from target-identity synthesis. A Gaussian-based emotion proxy avatar is trained once on a single identity and reused to generate audio-synchronized driving videos conditioned on emotion labels. The proxy video provides only motion and pose cues rather than target appearance. A one-shot portrait retargeting model then transfers these cues to arbitrary reference portraits while preserving identity, texture, and background information. To reduce inference cost, we cache reference appearance features at the initial denoising step and approximate later step-wise feature variations with low-rank residuals. Experiments show that our method improves emotion accuracy from 63.2% to 75.4% over a recent emotion-aware baseline, while maintaining competitive identity preservation and lip synchronization and running at 32 FPS.

Method Overview

A 3D Gaussian-based emotion proxy avatar first produces controllable emotional driving videos from audio and emotion conditions. The one-shot portrait motion driving module then transfers identity-independent proxy motion to arbitrary target portraits. Finally, the distillation acceleration module reduces redundant diffusion computation for efficient real-time portrait animation.
